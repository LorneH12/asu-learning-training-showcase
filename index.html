01 American Express · Enterprise enablementJira Cloud Migration Training View project

Synchronous · Global · Data-informed

Helping a global workforce prepare for a major technology transition

For a three-month Jira Data Center and Rally-to-Jira Cloud migration affecting a population of 60,000 users across seven groups, I coordinated and hosted 60+ live enablement sessions across six global time zones.

ChallengeCreate clear, scalable guidance for varied professional audiences while technical details and stakeholder questions continued to evolve.

Design decisionsCombined concise materials, SME demonstrations, leader context, moderated Q&A and structured follow-up communications.

Evidence and iterationSynthesized surveys, chats, transcripts and stakeholder input. AI-assisted theme analysis surfaced recurring questions and informed a shift toward more demonstrations and Q&A.

  • 6 time zones
  • 7 audience groups
02 Charles Schwab · Client and employee learningRole-Based Learning Journeys View project

Onboarding · Upskilling · Reinforcement

Turning a 200-page source binder into a guided learning experience

I organized dense operational content into role-based onboarding paths with practical milestones, guided practice and 30/60/90-day reinforcement.

ChallengeHelp employees find the right information at the moment they needed it.

Design decisionsUsed progressive disclosure, role-based paths, performance support and a SharePoint learning hub.

Value deliveredCreated a clearer path from orientation to confident performance while supporting updates and reuse.

03 Portfolio-wide course developmentA Repeatable Course Development Process View project

Analysis · Design · Development · Improvement

Using one clear process across different audiences and formats

My projects begin with the performance need—not the requested deliverable. I clarify the audience and evidence of success before selecting the content, modality and production approach.

01Define the needIdentify the audience, performance gap, constraints and desired evidence.

02Design the experienceSequence instruction, practice, support and assessment around real work.

03Build and reviewDevelop in short cycles with stakeholders and subject-matter experts.

04Launch and improvePrepare delivery, gather evidence and make targeted improvements.

04 Learning innovation and AIAI-Assisted Learning Design Workflow View project

Research · Synthesis · Human review

Using AI to accelerate analysis without outsourcing judgment

I use generative AI to help organize source material, identify patterns, draft alternatives and transform content across formats. Human review remains responsible for accuracy, context, quality and the final design decision.

Useful applicationsTheme analysis, first-draft outlines, content transformation, scenario options and production support.

Required controlsSource verification, privacy awareness, stakeholder review and clear accountability for the final work.

Design principleUse AI where it reduces mechanical effort so more attention can go to learner needs, business context and quality.
